**The normalize-once kernel did not move particle-only physics at rung 1 — measured, and it is
not because the roundings agree:** the reciprocal path (`n = d·RNE(2⁴⁸/r) >> 18`) disagrees with
the two-division normalize on **~1.6 % of (d, r) pairs** (2M-pair sample of the operand domain),
yet 1,400 sampled ticks of the 1k column reproduce rev 1 bit-for-bit. The 1-ulp `n` differences
are absorbed by the RNE layers between the gradient and the state write (`grad_term` →
`corr_comp30` → the once-per-substep `pos_from_xl` round). `--ladder 3` is the rung that can see
them, and does (tick 20). Structural confidence: High on the measurements, Medium on the
absorption arithmetic (not proven bit-by-bit).
